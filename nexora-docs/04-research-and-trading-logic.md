# 04 — Research and Trading Logic

## Purpose

Define how Nexora discovers, qualifies, researches, and presents opportunities and trade proposals.

## Status and scope

- **Status:** Not yet brainstormed
- **Scope:** Research and analytical behavior; deterministic calculations where applicable
- **Last updated:** 2026-10-01

## Confirmed decisions

- Discovery follows **Broad Market Discovery → Qualified Shortlist**.
- Research may cover company and market information, news, catalysts, technical context, price and volume behavior, important risks, and existing portfolio exposure.
- Clearly distinguish **FACT**, **ANALYSIS**, **SPECULATION**, and **UNCONFIRMED** information.
- Important claims should be traceable to sources when possible.
- Nexora should not present analysis as certainty about future market outcomes.
